Aiced Strategy has been built exclusively using Claude AI. I understand the complicated emotions regarding AI, so I have poured as much of myself into this as a person possibly could- but it should be known that every line of code, the UI, menus, all of it was built with Claude. I contributed only my thoughts. This game was inspired by an old mobile game called ICE. It was a simplistic strategy game about sending troops to bases, tactically working your way through the map and enemies until the entire match is yours. Simple upgrades, simple controls, but a deeply satisfying gameplay loop, with a cute appearance and audio that scratched my brain in a fun way as a bonus. This game was built with all of that in mind, I hope you enjoy it for what it is.

    What changed in the latest builds. This page is only in the test versions.

    Picture quality

    Auto lowers the picture quality by itself if your phone struggles. Lower levels make the picture less sharp and draw fewer particles, stars and snowflakes. Pick a level to hold it there, even if the game slows down.

    Selection ring (test): New is the style-shaped ring with a gauge for how much you send. Old is the dashed circle.

    Chip sound speed (test): New plays fewer, slower notes in a fight (up to about 5 a second). Old is the earlier speed.

    Sound weight: Full is the whole sound. Light and Lite use a shorter echo, fewer notes at once and fewer layers, for slower phones. Auto follows the picture level.

    How to Play

    One guided match that teaches the basics: sending troops, upgrades, special bases and taking a rival base. You can skip any step.

    Practice

    Rules

    • Goal. Take every base.
    • Send troops. Tap a base within your reach. To send only one base's troops, tap that base first, then tap where they should go. Tap your own base again to regroup. Double-tap to lock on: every new troop heads there until it's yours.
    • Take a base. Beat its defenders, then wear the base down. Deliver the final blow to claim it.
    • Get stronger. Eliminate troops to gain XP. A full bar earns 1 skill point to spend on Health, Speed or Damage. Claim bases to increase your army.
    • Look around. Pinch and drag. The map button shows a minimap, and a double-tap on it shows everything. Press and hold any base to see what it is.
    • Special bases. Fortress: more health. Forge: more troops. Beacon: more XP. Relay: faster troops.
