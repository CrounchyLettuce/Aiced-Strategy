  • You're blue. Tap a base along one of your colored lines and your swarm heads there. Tap your own base to pull troops back.
  • Double-tap a base to lock on. New troops keep heading there on their own until it's yours.
  • Kill a base's defenders, then wear the base itself down. Whoever lands the last hit takes it.
  • Kills fill your bar, and each full bar buys a level of health, speed or damage. More bases, bigger army.
  • Pinch to zoom and drag to look around.
