How to play

  • Pick your color before each game. Tap a base along one of your colored lines and your swarm heads there. Tap your own base to pull troops back.
  • Double-tap a base to lock on. New troops keep heading there on their own until it's yours.
  • Kill a base's defenders, then wear the base itself down. Whoever lands the last hit takes it.
  • Kills fill your bar, and each full bar buys a level of health, speed or damage. More bases means a bigger army.
  • The send button sets how much of your army each tap sends, so you can leave some behind to defend.
  • Map shapes change the terrain: islands joined by bridges, a ring around an empty middle, or a river with a few crossings. Bridges are chokepoints.
  • Pinch to zoom and drag to look around. The map button in the corner shows a minimap, and a double-tap on it pulls back to the whole battlefield.
  • Tap your army count to see every color's bases and upgrades.
